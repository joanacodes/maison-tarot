Three formats come back. **Free online readings**: one evening, a few people drawn at random, one reading each, in front of the others, knowing nothing about them. **In-person meetups**: in Paris and in the cities the House visits — we read, compare, talk. **Webinars**: one hour on one lens, open to everyone, with questions.

None of them is a consultation. All are offered for entertainment, and you remain the one who decides.
