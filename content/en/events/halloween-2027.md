Halloween is the night people expect the cards to frighten them. Come and see what a reading looks like instead.

You register; on the night, six people are drawn at random. Each gets a ten-minute reading in front of the others, and I know nothing about them: no name, no question. Afterwards each one says where they are, and we compare what landed and what missed. Everyone else watches and asks questions.

If Death or the Tower comes out, we read them like any other card, through their lenses. [What a Halloween reading is really like](/blog/halloween-tarot-reading/) explains why they're less frightening than they look.

I don't read on health, death, pregnancy, court cases or money, even for free. [The disclaimers](/disclaimers/) say why.
