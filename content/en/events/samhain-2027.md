One hour, online and free, on the [three-card Samhain spread](/blog/samhain-tarot/): what the year gave you, what you leave with the old season, and what you keep warm through the dark months.

We read it live. Volunteers draw their three cards and tell me nothing; I read them through their colours, numbers and elements, out loud, so you can follow the method rather than take my word for it. When something misses, I say so.

If your stocktake turns towards someone you've lost, the [ancestor spread](/blog/ancestor-tarot-spread/) is there for that, at your own pace. Questions at the end, as many as the hour allows.
