## How it works from where you are {#how}

[Sessions](/sessions/) are one-to-one and last an hour. [Workshops](/workshops/) last three hours, for twelve people at most. Both run live, by video, and when you book, every slot shows in your own time zone.

I teach from Paris. A workshop at 7 pm in Paris starts at 6 pm in London and, for most of the year, at 1 pm in New York and 10 am in Los Angeles.

## What it costs {#price}

A one-hour session is £60. A workshop is £45 per person, or £39.99 each when you come seven or more, which makes it a good evening with friends. Prices are in pounds on this site and in euros on the French one.

## Readings are free, and online {#readings}

Readings aren't for sale. They happen at [free online events](/events/): a few people drawn at random, each read in front of the others, with nothing said to me beforehand.

## In person: Paris and London {#in-person}

When we meet around a real table, it's in [Paris](/tarot-paris/), where I live, or in [London](/tarot-london/), where I used to. Anywhere else, the screen is the table, and it works: most of the three hundred readings I counted happened online.

## Before you book {#before}

Keep your own Rider-Waite-Smith deck at hand; [which edition to get](/blog/best-tarot-deck-for-beginners/) matters less than people think. Everything here is for adults, and some subjects stay off the table wherever you are: [what I don't read on](/disclaimers/#refusals).
