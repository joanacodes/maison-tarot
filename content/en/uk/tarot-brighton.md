## Learning tarot from Brighton {#from}

Brighton has the strongest alternative scene in England, which means the most people who already own a deck and have never been shown how to read it without a book. Everything the House teaches is taught online, by video, twelve people at most for the [workshops](/workshops/) and one-to-one for the [sessions](/sessions/). An online workshop at 7 pm Paris time is 6 PM in Brighton. Slots show automatically in your time zone, and prices in GBP.

You never pay for a reading: readings happen at [events](/events/), free. What you pay for is the lesson.

## Where to start {#start}

With one card, today, before any class: [the path](/learn-tarot/) explains the five lenses — [colours](/library/tarot-colours/), [elements](/library/four-elements/), [numerology](/library/numerology/), [energies](/library/feminine-masculine-energies/), [modes](/library/astrological-modes/) — and the article [How to read tarot cards for beginners](/blog/how-to-read-tarot-beginners/) gives you the sentence to say before your first reading. The [Readers' Guide](/guide/) is free.

## When the House comes to Brighton {#in-person}

In-person workshops are organised city by city, when there are enough people for a table of twelve. They appear on this page and on the [Events](/events/) page as soon as they exist; the Guide lets you know. If several of you in Brighton want a workshop, [write to us](/contact/): that's how they start.

## What the House doesn't do, in Brighton or anywhere {#refusals}

No readings on health, death, pregnancy, an ongoing court case or your finances; no "curse to lift". Everything is offered for entertainment, for people over 18. [The disclaimers, all in one place](/disclaimers/).
