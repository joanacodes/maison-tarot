The Sun in a career reading is success you can enjoy openly: a project that works, people who notice, a moment with nothing to hide. It’s one of the most generous cards in the deck for work. Its one real question is what you do once you’re seen, and how you stay grounded while the light is good.

{{< cards "ar19" >}}

## The Sun through its lenses {#lenses}

A huge sun with a human face fills the top of the card. Below, a naked child with flowers in its hair rides a white horse, arms open, holding a long red banner. Behind them runs a grey wall with sunflowers above it. No saddle, no reins, no clothes: joy with nothing to hide and nothing to grip.

The number: 19 becomes 1 + 9 = [10](/library/numerology/number-10/), the end of a cycle — in my notes, the corridor between two rooms, freedom, change, room to breathe. Its minus: it can be short-lived, and it tells you to start something else. In the Instagram story I use for the numbers, 10 is when the platform has become something else entirely and a new story begins with its own 1. At work, a success closes a chapter and opens the next one.

The element is Fire at its kindest: it warms, lights, and gets you moving. The archetype in my notes is a teenager full of life who thinks they can do anything — here, before anything destructive has crept in. The energy is masculine: acting, showing yourself, being on stage.

The colours: [yellow](/library/tarot-colours/yellow/) everywhere — joy, optimism, pride, self-expression; red on the banner for action; white on the horse for peace; grey on the wall for something solid behind you. The astrology is the Sun itself: the centre, the light you want to show. Every lens is on [the Sun’s own page](/library/tarot-cards/the-sun/).

## What the Sun says about work: success and visibility {#work}

At work, the Sun is the moment something works and people see it. A launch that lands. A presentation where the room leans in. A client who says yes and tells others. Recognition after a long effort. And sometimes something quieter and rarer: liking your job, and being glad on a Monday morning.

Visibility is the heart of it. The child has no clothes and nothing to hide: this is work that can be shown. The banner says you’re allowed to carry your own flag — to say “I did that” without apologising.

The Sun comes straight after the Moon in the deck, 19 after 18, and at work it often arrives after a long uncertain stretch: the restructure that ended, the probation that’s over, the project that finally shipped. I’ve written about [the Moon in a career reading](/blog/moon-tarot-career/) for that night before.

Say a small team ships a product after a hard year, and the Sun comes up. The card adds nothing mystical. **It says: notice it, thank the people who helped, and enjoy it before you plan the next thing.**

## The Sun reversed at work {#reversed}

Reversed, the Sun is the same light held back. A success you won’t let yourself enjoy, because “they’ll find out”. Credit you pass to others before anyone asks. A good moment kept for later, and later never comes.

Or it’s the Sun in excess: being seen becomes the point. The celebration post before the work is done, the good mood performed instead of felt, the banner bigger than the horse.

## The Sun next to other cards {#combinations}

{{< cards "wa06 ar10 pe09" >}}

Next to the Six of Wands — a rider crowned with a wreath, moving through a crowd — it’s success in public. My advice for 6 fits the pair: stay calm and humble when results flow.

Next to the Wheel of Fortune, another 10, it’s luck on your side. My note for the Wheel is that it’s up to you to turn it: create your own luck while the light is good.

Next to the Nine of Pentacles, a woman alone in her vineyard with a bird on her glove, it’s success you built and can enjoy quietly. My note for that card is gratitude for everything you have.

## “Will I get the promotion?” — and a kinder question {#question}

When this card comes up, people ask whether the good run will last: will I get the promotion, will the business take off, will I earn more? I understand. When things finally go well, you want to know they’ll stay that way.

Promotions are decided by people who aren’t in the room, and money — what you’ll earn, whether to invest — is one of the subjects I don’t read on ([what I don’t read, and why](/disclaimers/#refusals)). A card that promised a raise would turn the Sun into a debt you’d be waiting to collect.

A kinder question: “What made this work, and how do I keep doing it?” Or: “What would I like to be seen for at work?”

## Staying grounded while the light is good {#grounded}

The Sun’s minus is small but real, and I’ve written about it alongside [the happiest cards in the deck](/blog/happiest-tarot-cards/): a joy that has to be shown all the time gets tiring, and a 10 doesn’t last forever. Three things help.

1. Name who helped. The grey wall behind the child is everyone who held things up while you rode in front.
2. Write down what made it work while you still remember. It’s your recipe for the next 1.
3. Rest before the next project. A 10 is a corridor between two rooms, not a reason to start three things tomorrow.

Then lay one card for “what I want to grow next” and read only its colours. If you’d like to learn the colour lens properly, a [three-hour workshop](/workshops/) takes it in depth, with twelve people around a table.
