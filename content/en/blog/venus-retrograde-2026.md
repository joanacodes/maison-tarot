Venus is retrograde from 3 October to 14 November 2026 — 13 November if you live in the Americas — moving from Scorpio back into Libra. It's the planet astrology gives to love, pleasure and money, so you'll read a lot about returning exes this autumn. Here are the verified dates, what they're said to mean, and a calmer way to spend six weeks than waiting for a text.

## Venus retrograde 2026: the dates {#dates}

A Venus retrograde is the period when Venus, seen from Earth, appears to move backwards through the zodiac. It comes round about every nineteen months and lasts about six weeks: rarer than Mercury's, and twice as long.

| Date (UK time) | What happens |
|---|---|
| 31 August | Venus enters the "shadow", the stretch of sky it will cross three times |
| 3 October | Venus turns retrograde at 8° Scorpio |
| 23–24 October | Venus passes between the Earth and the Sun: the middle of the retrograde |
| 25 October | Venus backs out of Scorpio into Libra |
| 14 November | Venus turns direct at 22° Libra |
| 4 December | Venus re-enters Scorpio |
| 15 December | Venus leaves the shadow |

**The date people argue about, 13 or 14 November, is a time zone.** Venus turns direct at about half past midnight, London time, on 14 November, which is still the evening of 13 November in New York. Both are right. The UK astrologer [Jessica Davidson](https://jessicadavidson.co.uk/2026/08/17/venus-retrograde-in-scorpio-libra-2026/) and [Cafe Astrology](https://cafeastrology.com/events/venus-turns-direct-in-libra/) give the same turning points.

Two things make this one unusual. Mercury is retrograde too, from 24 October to 13 November, in Scorpio, and the two planets turn direct within about nine hours of each other ([the Mercury dates for 2026 and 2027](/blog/mercury-retrograde-dates/)). And Venus repeats its retrogrades in almost the same part of the sky every eight years: the last one in Scorpio and Libra ran from 5 October to 16 November 2018. If you kept a diary that autumn, it's worth reopening.

## What Venus retrograde is said to mean {#meaning}

In astrology, Venus describes what you love and how you love: taste, attraction, the way you bond, what you find beautiful — and, through Taurus, what you value, money included ([Venus in the Library](/library/astrology/venus/)). When it appears to go backwards, the usual reading is that these subjects get reviewed rather than started. Old relationships resurface, new ones are better not begun, big purchases and dramatic haircuts can wait.

This one starts in Scorpio — intimacy, trust, what's shared and what's hidden — and finishes in Libra, the sign of partnership and fairness. So the astrological story of autumn 2026 runs from "what do I really want from closeness?" to "is this relationship fair?".

That's a good pair of questions. You don't need a planet to ask them, and you don't need to wait for one to answer.

## A calm guide for your relationships {#calm-guide}

In my chart test, Venus held about a third of the time — like most placements, and not one of the strong three. So I use it as a prompt, never as a timetable. In practice:

- If an ex gets in touch, the question is the same as in any month: what has changed? If nothing has, the retrograde isn't a reason to try again. If something has, it isn't a reason not to.
- If a wedding, a first date or a move is booked in these weeks, keep it. Nothing in my numbers would justify shifting it.
- If you're tempted to leave or to stay because of the dates, don't hand that decision to a planet. It's yours, and you'll live with it long after 14 November.
- If you're waiting to hear from someone, I'd gently put the phone down. "Will they come back?" is a question about a person who isn't in the room, and the cards can't answer it for them. I've written about [why 'no contact' readings cost more than they give](/blog/no-contact-tarot-readings/).

**A retrograde can start a conversation about your relationship; it can't have it for you.** And if you feel the pull to look up someone's chart before a date, I'd leave it: [let the magic happen for the two of you](/blog/birth-chart-without-permission/).

## Reading Venus in the cards {#in-the-cards}

Venus's card is [the Empress](/library/tarot-cards/the-empress/); her symbol is drawn on the Empress's shield. The Empress feeds, gives and makes things grow — the wheat at her feet says so. Her minus side is that she can smother with care, or stop feeding altogether. The Two of Cups, ruled by Venus, shows two people exchanging cups as equals: Libra's fairness in one picture.

Turned into questions for these weeks, that gives a three-card spread:

1. Where am I feeding this relationship?
2. Where am I holding on too tight?
3. What have I stopped tending?

Draw it once — now, or on the day Venus turns direct — and write the answers down. Not every evening: take a break from your cards now and then, especially when you're waiting for someone.
