Search "no contact tarot" and you'll find thousands of readings, most of them cheap, all of them answering the same question: *what is my ex feeling right now?* It's one of the most popular formats on the internet, and one of the ones I refuse most firmly. Here's why, and what I'd read instead.

## What's being sold {#whats-sold}

You've cut contact with someone — or they've cut it with you — and the silence is unbearable. Into that silence a reader offers: they miss you. They regret it. They're about to reach out. Look, the Six of Cups, the Knight of Cups, the Lovers reversed — it's all there.

For 4,44 €, or 12, or a subscription. And it feels like relief, for an afternoon.

## Why it can't be true {#cant-be-true}

Not because tarot can't work — I think it can, under conditions I can describe — but because of what's being claimed. The reader has never met your ex. Your ex hasn't consented to being read. And the reading is being done through you: your grief, your hope, your description of the situation. What comes out is your inner life, dressed as theirs.

I'll say it more plainly. **No reader can tell you what another person feels.** Not me, not the woman on the video, not the phone line at twelve euros. Any reader who says they can is either fooling themselves or fooling you, and the cost is the same either way.

## What it costs {#what-it-costs}

Time. The weeks or months you'd spend grieving, deciding, rebuilding, get spent instead checking whether they've "moved closer" since Tuesday. I've watched people do a "no contact" reading every day for a month. That isn't tarot; [it's a dependency with a deck in it](/blog/why-you-should-quit-tarot-reading/), and the reading is feeding it on purpose, because a person who's stopped hoping stops paying.

And it costs you the honest version of the reading, which is much better.

## What I read instead {#what-i-read}

You. Where you actually are today — not the story about where you are, but the cards on the table, told nothing in advance. What you're holding onto. What you're avoiding deciding. The thing you're calling "waiting" that's really something else.

That reading has no Knight of Cups riding back in. It has a person, sitting in front of a picture, seeing where they actually stand. It's less pleasant for an afternoon and considerably more useful for a life. And it doesn't need anyone else's consent, because it's about the only person in the room.

## If you're in it right now {#if-youre-in-it}

Don't buy the reading. Not because I disapprove — because it will work exactly as designed, and what it's designed to do is keep you where you are.

If you need to ask the cards something, ask about you: what am I not looking at; what would I do this week if I stopped waiting. One card. Write down what comes. Then put the deck away for a while, and let the silence be silence. It's doing something, even when nobody is reading it.
