On 20 February 2026, Saturn and Neptune met at 0°45′ of Aries, the first degree of the zodiac. It was the most talked-about astrological event of the year: a cycle of about 36 years restarting on the zodiac's starting line. Eight months on, here's what was expected, what can honestly be said about it now, and the part I'd keep.

## What happened in the sky {#in-the-sky}

A conjunction is the moment when two planets, seen from Earth, sit at the same degree of the zodiac. Saturn and Neptune do it about every 36 years.

This one took a year to arrive. Neptune entered Aries on 30 March 2025, and Saturn followed in late May. In July 2025 they came within about a fifth of a degree of each other without quite meeting, then both backed into Pisces — Saturn on 1 September, Neptune on 22 October. They returned to Aries early in 2026, Neptune on 26 January and Saturn in mid-February, and met exactly on the 20th.

The previous Saturn–Neptune conjunction was in 1989, in Capricorn. The last one in Aries was in 1703, at about 11°. Bigger claims went round — "the first at 0° Aries in two thousand years", "the first since 4361 BC" — but they don't agree with each other and I couldn't check them, so I leave them out. [Cafe Astrology's page for the day](https://cafeastrology.com/events/february-20-2026) has the date; [Jessica Adams's list of past conjunctions](https://www.jessicaadams.com/2025/01/10/blog/the-saturn-neptune-conjunction-in-aries/) has 1703.

One more detail about the timing: the same fortnight held a solar eclipse on 17 February and the start of a Mercury retrograde on 26 February. With three signals in ten days, it becomes very hard to say which one did anything at all.

## What people expected {#expected}

In astrology, [Saturn](/library/astrology/saturn/) is structure, limits, reality, the hardest lesson. [Neptune](/library/astrology/neptune/) is the dream, ideals, illusion, things dissolving. Cafe Astrology described the pair as the dreamer and the realist. The two readings that circulated most were ideals finally taking solid form, and illusions about a person, a project or a system falling away.

The degree added the rest. 0° Aries is the point of the March equinox, where the tropical zodiac begins, and [Aries](/library/astrology/aries/) is cardinal Fire, the sign whose verb is "I am": the one that starts things. Put a 36-year cycle on that degree and you get the headlines — a new era, a turning point, a "great reset", as [the AstroTwins](https://astrostyle.com/saturn-neptune-alignment-february-20-2026-meaning/) put it.

The favourite precedent was 1989. The last exact meeting of that cycle came on 13 November 1989, four days after the Berlin Wall fell. It's a striking pair of dates, and a large part of why February 2026 drew so much attention.

## What it meant, eight months on {#what-it-meant}

I can't tell you what it did to the world, and I'd be careful with anyone who says they can. The forecasts were broad: endings, beginnings, illusions dropping, ideals taking shape. Across eight billion lives, a forecast that broad can't miss — something always ends, something always starts. A match found afterwards makes a good story; a forecast written before, specific enough to be wrong, makes a test.

**It also matters what my own numbers cover.** In my test, Saturn held 75 to 90% as a birth-chart placement: wherever it sat, people recognised their hardest lesson ([the three placements that held](/blog/saturn-lilith-chiron/)). That measures natal Saturn describing a person. It says nothing about Saturn's transits, and nothing about world events. I never tested those, and the numbers don't stretch that far.

So what did it mean? As a collective event, I don't know. As a prompt, it did something useful: for a few weeks, a lot of people asked which parts of their lives were built on a dream and which on something solid. That's a good question for any February.

## A test you can still run {#test}

The look-back that means something is your own, done the way I [tested my birth chart, box by box](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/):

1. Find what you wrote around February 2026: a notebook, a notes app, messages to a friend.
2. List what you expected: an ending, a start, a disillusion, a dream becoming real.
3. Next to each, tick or cross. Tick only if it happened in a way you couldn't have said of anyone.
4. Count. If an expectation missed, write it down as a miss. A miss is a miss.

If you read tarot, there's a picture for it too. Saturn's card is the World, Neptune's is [the Hanged Man](/library/tarot-cards/the-hanged-man/), and Aries belongs to the Emperor. Side by side: a cycle that closes, a view turned upside down, and the one who gives the first order.
