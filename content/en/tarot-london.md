## Learning from London {#from}

[One-to-one sessions](/sessions/) and [workshops](/workshops/) of up to twelve people run by video, in English, at times that suit London. Every slot shows in your own time when you book, so there's nothing to convert.

## Around a table in London {#in-person}

In-person workshops in London happen when enough people want the same table. They go on the [events page](/events/) first. If a few of you want one, [write to me](/contact/): that's how they usually start.

## Readings {#readings}

I don't sell readings, in London or anywhere else; they happen at the [free events](/events/). [What I don't read on](/disclaimers/#refusals) doesn't change with the city.
