## Who I am

My name is Joana. I read tarot from Paris, in French and in English, and mostly I teach it.

I came to all this through the back door. I wanted to understand why people believed in astrology — the way you’d want to understand flat-earthers; it was mockery. Two friends gave me a deck. I read for the people around me, and what I saw shut me up. So I did what you do when you don’t understand: I tested it. Three hundred readings, free, for strangers who gave me nothing to go on, counting what held and what didn’t. [The Method page](/method/) carries the tally.

What I like most isn’t reading. It’s getting someone to read for the first time and watching them be shocked by what they just said.

## What Maison Tarot believes

There are no Chosen Ones. This capacity is in everyone — easier for some, different for others — and it can be learned, like a language or an instrument. The cards are only a screen; the message is yours. One day you won’t need the cards at all. Tarot is kindergarten.

Don’t take my words as ultimate truth. Test it and experience it for yourself. No messiah, no prophet, no ranks among readers. We share what we’ve lived through, without much judgment; take it or leave it. We ask questions. The puzzle is a fresco, and it isn’t finished.

## Why “entertainment”?

Because I don’t know how this works. The line at the bottom of every page isn’t a loophole: it’s a promise not to pretend. What I know is a rate, and the conditions it holds under. That is exactly what I say, no more, no less.

And because some of what people bring to the cards isn’t for the cards: when it’s psychological, or psychiatric, the right door is a therapist’s, and I’ll tell you so.
