## The host

My name is {{< todo >}}[First Last — in hugo.toml]{{< /todo >}}. I read tarot from Paris, in French and in English, and mostly I teach it.

I came to all this through the back door. I wanted to understand why people believed in astrology — the way you’d want to understand flat-earthers; it was mockery. Two friends gave me a deck. I read for the people around me, and what I saw shut me up. So I did what you do when you don’t understand: I tested it. Three hundred free readings, for strangers who told me nothing, counting what held and what didn’t. The numbers are on the [Method](/method/) page.

What I like most isn’t reading. It’s getting someone to read for the first time and watching them be shocked by what they just said. That can’t be told; it has to be tried.

## What the House believes

There are no Chosen Ones. This capacity is in everyone — easier for some, different for others — and it can be learned, like a language or an instrument. The cards are only a screen; the message is yours. One day you won’t need the cards at all. Tarot is kindergarten.

Nobody here has the ultimate truth, until proven otherwise. No messiah, no prophet, no ranks among readers. We share what we’ve lived through, without much judgment; take it or leave it. We ask questions. The puzzle is a fresco, and it isn’t finished.

## Why “entertainment”?

Because I don’t know how this works. The line at the bottom of every page isn’t a loophole: it’s a promise not to pretend. I know it works at a certain rate, under certain conditions. That is exactly what I say, no more, no less.

And because not everything is about spirits. Sometimes it’s psychological, sometimes psychiatric. In those cases the right door isn’t mine, and I’ll tell you so.
