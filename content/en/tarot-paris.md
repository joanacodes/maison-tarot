[Sessions](/sessions/) are one-to-one; [workshops](/workshops/) take twelve people at most. Both run by video most of the time, and around a real table in Paris when enough people ask for one. English or French: the group decides.

## Meeting in Paris {#in-person}

In-person dates go on the [events page](/events/) first. If you're a few English speakers in Paris and want a workshop together, [write to me](/contact/).

Readings aren't for sale here either: they happen at the [free events](/events/), where you tell me nothing and I say what comes.
