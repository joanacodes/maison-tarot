## What you won’t do here

You won’t memorise 78 meanings. You won’t cleanse your deck with sage. You won’t learn to sell readings. You won’t get a certificate.

## What you will do

You’ll read. From the first hour to the last minute. You’ll get it wrong, and we’ll look at why together — that’s where most of the learning is. You’ll leave with one thing you know how to do, and the urge to know more.
