## What blue says {#definition}

**Emotions, making pure, water, vitality.** Feminine energy. That's the [colour grid](/library/tarot-colours/) in one line; the rest of the page says where to look and how to read.

## Where the colour appears in the deck {#where}

The High Priestess's robe, the Empress's cloak, the water in every Cups card, Temperance's robe, the river at the Emperor's feet, the background of the Knight of Cups. Blue is wherever emotion is.

## How to read it {#reading}

Blue says **emotion, purity, vitality, what flows**. A blue card is about the inner world — a relationship, a grief, a peace regained. It's the colour of [Water](/library/four-elements/water-element/), with [feminine](/library/feminine-masculine-energies/) energy: what hides, what carries.

## The flip side {#flip-side}

Blue's flip side is drowning: emotion that submerges, melancholy, what has seeped in too long without being looked at. Dark blue with no yellow: the card needs light.

## With the other lenses {#lenses}

Colour gives the tone; the [element](/library/four-elements/) says the field, the [number](/library/numerology/) the step. Lay all three and the card speaks. [The complete path](/learn-tarot/) puts them in order.
