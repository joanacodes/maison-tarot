## What orange says {#definition}

**Creativity, dryness, libido.** That's the [colour grid](/library/tarot-colours/) in one line; the rest of the page says where to look and how to read.

## Where the colour appears in the deck {#where}

The ground and sky of many Wands — the Three, the Five, the Seven — the Knight of Wands' robe, the Chariot's canopy, the background of the Wheel of Fortune, the Devil's flames. Wherever the world heats up.

## How to read it {#reading}

Orange says **creativity, libido, heat** — the energy that makes. An orange card is about a desire looking for a shape: a project, an attraction, an urge to do. It's the colour of the [Wands](/library/four-elements/fire-element/) above all.

## The flip side {#flip-side}

Orange's flip side is dryness: cracked ground, heat without water, desire that comes to nothing. A lot of orange and no blue: the card is thirsty.

## With the other lenses {#lenses}

Colour gives the tone; the [element](/library/four-elements/) says the field, the [number](/library/numerology/) the step. Lay all three and the card speaks. [The complete path](/learn-tarot/) puts them in order.
