## What black says {#definition}

**Absence, energy absorbed, void, negative thoughts, emptying out, reality.** That's the [colour grid](/library/tarot-colours/) in one line; the rest of the page says where to look and how to read.

## Where the colour appears in the deck {#where}

The Five of Cups' cloak, the Devil's background, the Tower's sky, Death's armour, the Queen of Wands' cat, the High Priestess's left pillar. Black almost never dresses someone who's doing well.

## How to read it {#reading}

Black says **absence, negative thoughts, emptying out, reality**. A black card is about what's missing, what weighs, or what someone refuses to see. But also about what is *true*: black is the colour of the bare thing, without the varnish.

## The flip side {#flip-side}

Black has a right side and a wrong side. Right side up, it's the useful void — making room, looking at reality. Wrong side up, it's the hole where energy disappears: depression, denial, the Tower you didn't see coming. The neighbouring card says which.

## With the other lenses {#lenses}

Colour gives the tone; the [element](/library/four-elements/) says the field, the [number](/library/numerology/) the step. Lay all three and the card speaks. [The complete path](/learn-tarot/) puts them in order.
