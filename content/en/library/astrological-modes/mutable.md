## What the mode says {#definition}

The mutable signs end the seasons: Gemini spring, Virgo summer, Sagittarius autumn, Pisces winter. Everything is already becoming something else. It's the energy of passage, of translation — and of blur, when it has nothing to hold on to.

## The four signs and their cards {#signs}

| Sign | Element | Card | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gemini | Air | [The Lovers](/library/tarot-cards/the-lovers/) | Choice, communication |
| Virgo | Earth | [The Hermit](/library/tarot-cards/the-hermit/) | Analysis, withdrawing to understand |
| Sagittarius | Fire | [Temperance](/library/tarot-cards/temperance/) | Blending, measure, travel |
| Pisces | Water | [The Moon](/library/tarot-cards/the-moon/) | Intuition, illusion, dream |

## The advice {#advice}

Change without getting lost. Mutable at its best is Temperance: it blends two things and makes a third; at its worst it's the Moon with no shore — everything moves and nothing settles. The advice of [10](/library/numerology/number-10/) — breathe before the next 1 — is its advice too.

## In a reading {#reading}

A spread dominated by mutable says: **it's changing, and the next shape isn't here yet.** An end of season, a translation, a hesitation between two versions. A good sign if the situation was stuck; worrying if it needed to hold. The mode says the tempo; the [element](/library/four-elements/) says what's changing.

## The three modes {#modes}

To start, to hold, to change. The other two: [cardinal](/library/astrological-modes/cardinal/) · [fixed](/library/astrological-modes/fixed/). And the [full table of signs](/library/astrology/) to see all three at once.
