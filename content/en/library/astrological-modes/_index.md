| [Mutable](/library/astrological-modes/mutable/) | To change | Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces | The Lovers, the Hermit, Temperance, the Moon |

## How to use this lens {#how}

The mode is read after the first three lenses. [Colours](/library/tarot-colours/) give the tone, the [element](/library/four-elements/) the field, the [number](/library/numerology/) the step; the mode says whether the thing is starting, lasting or transforming. Three Emperors in a row in a life is someone who always restarts; a Strength that won't move is someone who holds — maybe too much.
