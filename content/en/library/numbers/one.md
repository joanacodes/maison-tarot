## What 1 says {#definition}

**One is the initiator.** It's the first number, the one everything starts from, and it knows nothing of what comes next. In the [cycle of numbers](/library/numbers/) it opens: an idea that just arrived, an urge that hasn't been tested, a new resource whose exact value nobody knows.

The right phrase is **rough diamond**. It already has all its value — and it's useful for nothing yet. It hasn't been cut, it has no shape, nobody can wear it. That's the state of everything that begins: precious and unusable, at the same time.

## The advice of 1 {#advice}

When 1 comes out, the advice fits in one word: **humility**.

- Stay humble: what you're holding is promising, not accomplished.
- Get ready to learn: the start of a cycle is the moment you know the least, and that's normal.
- Don't tell yourself the ending: the person who draws a 1 usually already has the win in mind. They're on page one.

## An example everyone knows {#example}

In 2010, Kevin Systrom and Mike Krieger built a small location-sharing app called Burbn. It didn't really work. They kept the only feature people used — sharing a photo — and renamed it Instagram.

At that moment they had a 1: an idea that fits in the hand, no proof it's worth anything, and only one right move available — staying a student of what they'd just launched. The rest of the cycle, from [2](/library/numbers/two/) (investors signing) to [10](/library/numbers/ten/) (a platform nobody recognises any more), is the story of the same company. That's why the House uses Instagram as its running example: you already know the ending, so you can see the cycle.

## The five cards that carry 1 {#cards}

You don't have to memorise these five. You already know they're about a beginning; the [element](/library/elements/) tells you in which area.

- [The Magician](/library/cards/the-magician/) — major arcana I. The beginning that has every tool on the table and no result yet.
- [Ace of Wands](/library/cards/ace-of-wands/) — 1 + Fire: an urge, a drive, an idea that's growing.
- [Ace of Cups](/library/cards/ace-of-cups/) — 1 + Water: a new feeling, a relationship starting, a heart opening again.
- [Ace of Swords](/library/cards/ace-of-swords/) — 1 + Air: a clear idea, a decision that just landed, a truth breaking through.
- [Ace of Pentacles](/library/cards/ace-of-pentacles/) — 1 + Earth: a concrete opportunity, money arriving, a project that finally has a base.

## In a reading {#reading}

A 1 in a spread says: **something has just started, or is about to**. Look at the card next to it to know whether the person knows. Next to a [Ten](/library/numbers/ten/), it's the page turning. Next to a [Five](/library/numbers/five/), it's a start already under dispute. On its own, it's an invitation not to run.

A whole spread with no 1 in it says something else: nothing is starting here, everything is already under way. That's information too.
