## What 7 says {#definition}

**7 is analysis.** Patience, calm, negotiations, harvesting. Seven is analysis: you look at what you have, and decide what's next. In the [cycle of numbers](/library/numerology/) it comes after 6 and prepares 8.

## The advice of 7 {#advice}

Remember that everything is temporary, and that the situation you're in — favourable or not — will move at some point. Seven is for thinking, not for concluding.

## An example everyone knows {#example}

Facebook decides to change Instagram's model and make it an advertising platform. Neither triumph nor fall: it's the negotiating table, where you choose what the thing will become.

## The five cards that carry 7 {#cards}

- [The Chariot](/library/tarot-cards/the-chariot/) — major arcana 7.
- [Seven of Wands](/library/tarot-cards/seven-of-wands/) — 7 + Fire.
- [Seven of Cups](/library/tarot-cards/seven-of-cups/) — 7 + Water.
- [Seven of Swords](/library/tarot-cards/seven-of-swords/) — 7 + Air.
- [Seven of Pentacles](/library/tarot-cards/seven-of-pentacles/) — 7 + Earth.

## In a reading {#reading}

A 7 in a spread says: **take the time to look before deciding.** The Seven of Pentacles contemplates its harvest, the Seven of Cups hesitates between illusions, the Seven of Swords acts on the sly, the Seven of Wands holds its ground. The Chariot is the 7 of will: it moves because it has analysed where it's going.
