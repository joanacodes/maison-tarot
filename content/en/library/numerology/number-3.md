## What 3 says {#definition}

**3 is cadence.** The machine running, the contract working, siblings on good terms. Three is the cadence found: it works, and it shows. In the [cycle of numbers](/library/numerology/) it comes after 2 and prepares 4.

## The advice of 3 {#advice}

Make sure you finish a project before starting another. Three gives momentum; momentum gives ideas; ideas scatter.

## An example everyone knows {#example}

Instagram's launch works. People sign up, post, come back. Nothing is won yet (see 5), but for the first time the thing turns without being pushed. The thread running through this whole section is the story of Instagram, from [1](/library/numerology/number-1/) to 10: you know how it ends, so you can see the cycle.

## The five cards that carry 3 {#cards}

You don't have to memorise them. The number says the step; the [element](/library/four-elements/) says the plane it plays on.

- [The Empress](/library/tarot-cards/the-empress/) — major arcana 3.
- [Three of Wands](/library/tarot-cards/three-of-wands/) — 3 + Fire.
- [Three of Cups](/library/tarot-cards/three-of-cups/) — 3 + Water.
- [Three of Swords](/library/tarot-cards/three-of-swords/) — 3 + Air.
- [Three of Pentacles](/library/tarot-cards/three-of-pentacles/) — 3 + Earth.

## In a reading {#reading}

A 3 in a spread says: **it works, and it shows.** The Three of Cups celebrates, the Three of Pentacles builds together, the Three of Wands watches its ships leave, the Three of Swords — the only painful 3 — shows that when three hearts are involved, the cadence can also break. The Empress is the 3 that produces: fertility in the widest sense.
