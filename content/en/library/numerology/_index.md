A detail people miss about 10: from the outside, you might think the person is down. In reality the butterfly is already far away, out of its chrysalis.

## Why Instagram and not a Greek myth {#why-instagram}

Because you know the ending. Tarot books illustrate the numbers with allegories nobody has lived; the House takes a story everyone watched happen. When you know how it ends you see the whole cycle at once — and then you recognise it in your own life, which doesn't have a known ending yet.

## Reading a number in a spread {#reading}

The number says *where* the situation is; the [element](/library/four-elements/) says *what* it's about. A Three of Cups and a Three of Pentacles tell the same step — the machine running — in two areas: relationships, matter. That's why you don't learn 40 numbered minor cards; you learn ten numbers and four houses.

Two numbers side by side read as a movement: a 1 next to a 10 is a page turning; a 5 next to a 6 is a fight being won. And a spread with no low numbers says something too: nothing is starting here.

## The four ranks {#the-ranks}

The Page is the student, learning and carrying the message. The Knight acts, leaves, sometimes overdoes it. The Queen masters from the inside: she welcomes and she holds. The King masters from the outside: he decides. They're temperaments more than steps, and each has its page in this section.
