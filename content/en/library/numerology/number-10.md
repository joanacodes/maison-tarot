## What 10 says {#definition}

**10 is transition.** Freedom, change, travel, breathing. Ten — or 0 — is the transition: what was is finishing, what comes has no name yet. In the [cycle of numbers](/library/numerology/) it comes after 9 and prepares 1.

## The advice of 10 {#advice}

Don't mistake the moult for the fall. Ten asks you to breathe, to let the old shape go, and not to run straight at the next 1.

## An example everyone knows {#example}

Instagram gradually changes its designs; people no longer recognise the platform, because it has become another one entirely. The cycle is closed. What starts now is another story — with its own 1.

## The five cards that carry 10 {#cards}

- [Wheel of Fortune](/library/tarot-cards/wheel-of-fortune/) — major arcana X.
- [Ten of Wands](/library/tarot-cards/ten-of-wands/) — 10 + Fire.
- [Ten of Cups](/library/tarot-cards/ten-of-cups/) — 10 + Water.
- [Ten of Swords](/library/tarot-cards/ten-of-swords/) — 10 + Air.
- [Ten of Pentacles](/library/tarot-cards/ten-of-pentacles/) — 10 + Earth.

## In a reading {#reading}

A 10 in a spread says: **a page is turning.** The Ten of Cups closes the cycle in joy, the Ten of Pentacles hands it on, the Ten of Wands carries it to the end, bent double, the Ten of Swords finishes it on the ground — the worst image in the deck, and yet there's dawn at the back. The Wheel of Fortune is the 10 that turns: nothing stays up, nothing stays down.
