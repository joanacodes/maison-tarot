## What 9 says {#definition}

**9 is home stretch.** Home stretch, introspection, patience, last efforts. Nine is the end preparing itself. In the [cycle of numbers](/library/numerology/) it comes after 8 and prepares 10.

## The advice of 9 {#advice}

Don't stop a hundred metres from the finish. Nine asks you to hold a little longer, and to look inside at what the cycle has changed before closing it.

## An example everyone knows {#example}

Instagram redesigns its whole platform and promotes nothing but Reels. It isn't another app yet (that's the 10), but everything that defined it is ending. The thread running through this whole section is the story of Instagram, from [1](/library/numerology/number-1/) to 10: you know how it ends, so you can see the cycle.

## The five cards that carry 9 {#cards}

You don't have to memorise them. The number says the step; the [element](/library/four-elements/) says the plane it plays on.

- [The Hermit](/library/tarot-cards/the-hermit/) — major arcana 9.
- [Nine of Wands](/library/tarot-cards/nine-of-wands/) — 9 + Fire.
- [Nine of Cups](/library/tarot-cards/nine-of-cups/) — 9 + Water.
- [Nine of Swords](/library/tarot-cards/nine-of-swords/) — 9 + Air.
- [Nine of Pentacles](/library/tarot-cards/nine-of-pentacles/) — 9 + Earth.

## In a reading {#reading}

A 9 in a spread says: **you're almost there, and it shows.** The Nine of Pentacles enjoys alone what it built, the Nine of Cups has what it wanted, the Nine of Wands stands, wounded, the Nine of Swords can't sleep from thinking about it. The Hermit is the 9 that looks inward: the lantern before the last step.
