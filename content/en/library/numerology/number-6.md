## What 6 says {#definition}

**6 is expansion (3 exalted).** Success, triumph, speed multiplied. Six is expansion — 3 exalted, the machine that not only runs but accelerates. In the [cycle of numbers](/library/numerology/) it comes after 5 and prepares 7.

## The advice of 6 {#advice}

Stay calm and humble in the flow of favourable results. Six goes to the head; a swollen head makes the mistakes of 7.

## An example everyone knows {#example}

Facebook decides to invest. The trial of 5 is passed, and what threatened the company becomes what propels it. Everything goes faster, everything goes better — and nobody reads the conditions any more. The thread running through this whole section is the story of Instagram, from [1](/library/numerology/number-1/) to 10: you know how it ends, so you can see the cycle.

## The five cards that carry 6 {#cards}

You don't have to memorise them. The number says the step; the [element](/library/four-elements/) says the plane it plays on.

- [The Lovers](/library/tarot-cards/the-lovers/) — major arcana 6.
- [Six of Wands](/library/tarot-cards/six-of-wands/) — 6 + Fire.
- [Six of Cups](/library/tarot-cards/six-of-cups/) — 6 + Water.
- [Six of Swords](/library/tarot-cards/six-of-swords/) — 6 + Air.
- [Six of Pentacles](/library/tarot-cards/six-of-pentacles/) — 6 + Earth.

## In a reading {#reading}

A 6 in a spread says: **it's succeeding, and it's fast.** The Six of Wands parades as the winner, the Six of Pentacles has enough to give, the Six of Cups finds the sweetness of before, the Six of Swords crosses to a calmer shore. The Lovers are the 6 of the happy choice: union — and the choice it implies.
