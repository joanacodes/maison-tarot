## What 8 says {#definition}

**8 is empire (4 exalted).** Legacy, assured stability, prosperity. Eight is the empire — 4 exalted, structure become power. In the [cycle of numbers](/library/numerology/) it comes after 7 and prepares 9.

## The advice of 8 {#advice}

Mastery is proved by the repeated gesture, not the title. Eight advises you to keep working as if nothing were secured — because that's how it stayed secured.

## An example everyone knows {#example}

Instagram, now at Facebook, has become an empire generating billions a year. It's no longer a bet or a negotiation: it's infrastructure. Nobody asks whether it works; they ask what it does to the world.

## The five cards that carry 8 {#cards}

- [Strength](/library/tarot-cards/strength/) — major arcana 8.
- [Eight of Wands](/library/tarot-cards/eight-of-wands/) — 8 + Fire.
- [Eight of Cups](/library/tarot-cards/eight-of-cups/) — 8 + Water.
- [Eight of Swords](/library/tarot-cards/eight-of-swords/) — 8 + Air.
- [Eight of Pentacles](/library/tarot-cards/eight-of-pentacles/) — 8 + Earth.

## In a reading {#reading}

An 8 in a spread says: **it's solid, and it lasts.** The Eight of Pentacles works piece after piece, the Eight of Wands flies — speed mastered — the Eight of Cups leaves what's built to look for something else, the Eight of Swords thinks itself prisoner of what it built. Strength is the 8 of inner mastery: the gentleness that holds the lion.
