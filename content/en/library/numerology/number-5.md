## What 5 says {#definition}

**5 is trial.** Conflict, test, war or battle, elaborating, setting up, challenge, making do with what's left. Five is the trial that measures. In the [cycle of numbers](/library/numerology/) it comes after 4 and prepares 6.

## The advice of 5 {#advice}

Avoid pointless conflicts. Be honest, with yourself and with the situation. Five isn't won by telling yourself there's no problem.

## An example everyone knows {#example}

Facebook arrives to buy Instagram — or, another version of the same 5, IGTV flops and will be pulled. A threat from outside, or a bet lost inside: either way, you find out what holds. The thread running through this whole section is the story of Instagram, from [1](/library/numerology/number-1/) to 10: you know how it ends, so you can see the cycle.

## The five cards that carry 5 {#cards}

You don't have to memorise them. The number says the step; the [element](/library/four-elements/) says the plane it plays on.

- [The Hierophant](/library/tarot-cards/the-hierophant/) — major arcana 5.
- [Five of Wands](/library/tarot-cards/five-of-wands/) — 5 + Fire.
- [Five of Cups](/library/tarot-cards/five-of-cups/) — 5 + Water.
- [Five of Swords](/library/tarot-cards/five-of-swords/) — 5 + Air.
- [Five of Pentacles](/library/tarot-cards/five-of-pentacles/) — 5 + Earth.

## In a reading {#reading}

A 5 in a spread says: **there's a conflict, and the question is what you do with it.** The Five of Wands brawls, the Five of Swords wins while losing allies, the Five of Cups mourns what's spilled instead of seeing what's standing, the Five of Pentacles walks in the snow past a lit window. The Hierophant is the 5 of institutions: the trial of the rule.
