## What 4 says {#definition}

**4 is stability (2 exalted).** The home, being at home, the structure. Four is stability achieved — 2 exalted, the alliance become a foundation. In the [cycle of numbers](/library/numerology/) it comes after 3 and prepares 5.

## The advice of 4 {#advice}

Stay flexible. Open up to possibilities you hadn't considered. A structure that holds too well ends up letting no air in.

## An example everyone knows {#example}

Instagram is a big success, fast. The app has a shape, a team, habits. It's solid — and that's exactly the moment a company starts believing it will never change again. The thread running through this whole section is the story of Instagram, from [1](/library/numerology/number-1/) to 10: you know how it ends, so you can see the cycle.

## The five cards that carry 4 {#cards}

You don't have to memorise them. The number says the step; the [element](/library/four-elements/) says the plane it plays on.

- [The Emperor](/library/tarot-cards/the-emperor/) — major arcana 4.
- [Four of Wands](/library/tarot-cards/four-of-wands/) — 4 + Fire.
- [Four of Cups](/library/tarot-cards/four-of-cups/) — 4 + Water.
- [Four of Swords](/library/tarot-cards/four-of-swords/) — 4 + Air.
- [Four of Pentacles](/library/tarot-cards/four-of-pentacles/) — 4 + Earth.

## In a reading {#reading}

A 4 in a spread says: **it holds.** The Four of Wands celebrates a home, the Four of Pentacles clutches what it has, the Four of Cups is bored in its comfort, the Four of Swords rests. The Emperor is the 4 that structures: the rule, the throne, the frame. Look at whether the stability protects or confines.
