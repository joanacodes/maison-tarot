## What 2 says {#definition}

**2 is cooperation.** Signing a contract, cooperation between two parties, binding. Two is the number of the alliance — and of the caution it asks for. In the [cycle of numbers](/library/numerology/) it comes after 1 and prepares 3.

## The advice of 2 {#advice}

Read the contract properly. Weigh the pros and cons. Be steady before you commit: two rewards those who take their time, not those who sign fast.

## An example everyone knows {#example}

Systrom and Krieger sign with investors. The idea exists (the 1), and now someone else is putting money, time and conditions into it. It's the first moment you can lose something other than your own time — hence the advice to read the contract. The thread running through this whole section is the story of Instagram, from [1](/library/numerology/number-1/) to 10: you know how it ends, so you can see the cycle.

## The five cards that carry 2 {#cards}

You don't have to memorise them. The number says the step; the [element](/library/four-elements/) says the plane it plays on.

- [The High Priestess](/library/tarot-cards/the-high-priestess/) — major arcana 2.
- [Two of Wands](/library/tarot-cards/two-of-wands/) — 2 + Fire.
- [Two of Cups](/library/tarot-cards/two-of-cups/) — 2 + Water.
- [Two of Swords](/library/tarot-cards/two-of-swords/) — 2 + Air.
- [Two of Pentacles](/library/tarot-cards/two-of-pentacles/) — 2 + Earth.

## In a reading {#reading}

A 2 in a spread says: **there are two parties, and the bond between them is the question.** A couple, a partner, a choice between two options held at equal distance. The Two of Cups ties, the Two of Swords holds back, the Two of Wands looks far before leaving, the Two of Pentacles juggles. The High Priestess keeps the bond secret: a 2 that doesn't say yet.
