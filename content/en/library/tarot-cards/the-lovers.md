## What’s on the card {#the-card}

A naked man and a naked woman stand apart in the open, under a great angel. The angel spreads red wings over them from a cloud, arms open, with a huge sun blazing behind. Behind the man stands a tree whose leaves are flames; behind the woman, a tree heavy with fruit, a snake coiled around its trunk. Between them, in the distance, rises a single tall mountain.

Follow the gazes. The man looks at the woman. The woman looks up at the angel. Nobody is looking at the same thing.

What’s missing: contact — their hands don’t touch. No ring, no crowd, no ceremony. **The drawing is about a choice being made under someone’s gaze, more than about a romance already sealed.**

## By number: 6 {#number}

**6 — six — harmony regained, exchange.** The [number 6](/library/numerology/number-6/) is 3 exalted: the machine is launched and fast, and harmony comes back after the trial of 5. On this card the 6 is the exchange between two people — and the choice it implies, because agreeing with someone means saying yes to them and no to something else.

In the story of Instagram on my numerology pages, 6 is Facebook deciding to invest: the threat of the 5 becomes the engine, everything speeds up, and nobody reads the conditions any more. That’s worth remembering with the Lovers. The advice of 6 is to stay calm and humble while it’s going well; a happy union still deserves a look at its terms.

## By element: Air {#element}

The Lovers are an [Air](/library/four-elements/air-element/) card, and that surprises people. If this were pure feeling, it would be Water. Air is the decision, thinking, communication — the conversation that settles things.

So the card is a choice more than a romance: two people talking, weighing, deciding where they’re going. The plus of Air is clarity; it cuts cleanly. The minus is the mind that goes round and round, or the conversation that gets twisted on the way.

## By colours {#colours}

Red and yellow dominate the top of the card. In [my colour grid](/library/tarot-colours/red/), red is passion, action, impulse; on the angel’s wings and in the flames of the man’s tree, it’s an energy that pushes. Yellow on the sun: joy, will, a choice made in broad daylight.

Read before the card: desire, will, and a decision taken in full light.

## By astrology: Gemini {#astrology}

In the Golden Dawn tradition, the Lovers go with [Gemini](/library/astrology/gemini/): “I think”, ruled by Mercury, a mutable sign that changes in order to communicate. Twins, two of something — the card’s pair. At its best Gemini is the great reporter who says exactly what was meant; at its worst it’s a game of Chinese whispers, where the version going round is no longer the true one. A useful nuance for a love card: what’s being said, and by whom. Optional, like all the astrology here.

## In a reading {#reading}

Upright, the Lovers say: a choice, made with someone or about something you love. Sometimes it’s a relationship; often it’s a crossroads — two paths, two offers, two versions of your life — and the card asks you to choose with your eyes open. In love it’s [a choice more than a romance](/blog/lovers-tarot-love/); at work it’s choosing a path ([the Lovers in career](/blog/lovers-tarot-career/)).

People often hope this card will tell them whether someone loves them. I understand the wish. But I read the person in front of me, not someone who isn’t in the room, so the Lovers speak about your choice rather than another person’s feelings ([more on reading about someone else](/blog/reading-tarot-about-someone-else/)).

Reversed, the choice is postponed or made for the wrong reasons. Either the person stays at the crossroads, unable to give anything up, or the exchange breaks down — two people talking past each other, each sure they’ve been clear.

Next to the [Two of Cups](/library/tarot-cards/two-of-cups/), two people exchanging cups, the bond is mutual and the feeling is there. Next to [the Devil](/library/tarot-cards/the-devil/) — the same naked couple, this time chained to a pedestal — the question is whether the bond is a choice or a habit; on that card, the chains are loose enough to lift off. **The Lovers don’t tell you whom to choose; they show you that you are choosing.**

## A reading, as an example {#example}

Say someone sits down, tells you nothing, and draws the Lovers next to the [Two of Pentacles](/library/tarot-cards/two-of-pentacles/): a young man juggling two pentacles joined by a ribbon in a figure of eight, with ships riding high waves behind him.

Lenses first. Numbers: 6 then 2, a choice that should bring harmony, next to a 2 that is still weighing. Elements: Air then Earth, a decision that touches something concrete — money, time, a job, a place to live. Colours: the Lovers’ red and yellow, a decision in full light, next to a card in motion.

The sentence I’d say out loud: “There’s a choice in front of you, and you’ve been juggling both options so you don’t have to pick one.”

Then I’d check: is there something you’re keeping two of, so you don’t have to give either up? If it lands, the next question is which one they’d keep if they had to decide tonight. If it doesn’t, that’s a miss, and I say so plainly.
