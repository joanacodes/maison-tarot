## What Earth stands for

Matter, prosperity, and everything that matters to you. Pentacles — also called Coins — aren’t only about money: they’re about **what has value**, and the result of work. A home, a salary, a relationship that feeds you, a healthy body: Earth.

It’s an element with mostly [feminine](/library/feminine-masculine-energies/) energy: protective, stable, the one who leads quietly and is mother to all the life and growth around her.

## Its strength, and the flip side

Earth’s stability means tenacity, perseverance, endurance. The flip side is obvious: **stubborn, dug in**, slow to change its mind even when reality insists. When you draw a lot of Pentacles, ask whether the person is holding steady — or holding on.

## Keywords

Nurturer · matter · value · finances · the result of work.

## The archetype

An artist’s manager. Works in the shadows, constantly and solemnly, and it’s thanks to him the bills get paid. But he can stop listening to the artist and do things his way, because his vision is “the right one” — the one that pays the most. Sometimes at the expense of what the artist set out to do.

## A reading, as an example

You draw the Ten of Pentacles for someone who has told you nothing. Before looking up what the card “means”, you already have two things: *ten* — the end of a cycle, the transition ([numbers](/library/numerology/)) — and *Earth* — what has value, what has been built. You can already talk about an inheritance, a house, an estate changing hands. You memorised nothing.
