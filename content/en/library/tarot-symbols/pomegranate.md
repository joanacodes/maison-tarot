## What the pomegranate says {#definition}

From the outside, a pomegranate looks plain: a hard reddish skin, a little crown on top. Cut it open and it’s packed — hundreds of seeds in a juice that stains everything it touches. **The pomegranate says: there’s more inside than the surface lets on, and getting at it takes a knife or patience.** Think of the quiet colleague who turns out to know the whole history of the company.

It has stood for plenty and fertility for a very long time, precisely because of all those seeds.

## Where it appears in the deck {#where}

On [the High Priestess](/library/tarot-cards/the-high-priestess/), the veil hung between the two pillars behind her is printed with palm leaves and pomegranates, several of them cut open to show red seeds. The fruit sits right behind her head, on the very thing that hides what’s behind her. [The veil](/library/tarot-symbols/veil/) has its own page.

On the Empress, the white dress is printed all over with pomegranates in red and green. She sits back on her cushions in a field of wheat, a waterfall behind her. Here the fruit isn’t hidden: she wears it.

Two cards, next to each other in the deck: 2 and 3. Same fruit, opposite gestures. One keeps the seeds behind a veil; the other has them all over her dress.

## How to read it {#reading}

When the pomegranate comes up, ask what in the situation hasn’t been opened yet. Behind the High Priestess, it’s knowledge kept: in my notes she’s still water, what we don’t see, a façade with the rest hidden. On the Empress, it’s abundance offered: she feeds, gives, creates, nourishes.

Lenses: the High Priestess is [Water](/library/four-elements/water-element/) and the Empress is Earth. The same fruit says “what you feel and haven’t said” on one card, and “what you’ve grown and can share” on the other. Numbers: 2 is the contract and the weighing up; [3](/library/numerology/number-3/) is the machine running, the project that works.

Say someone draws the High Priestess while deciding whether to accept a job. The pomegranates on the veil might say: there’s more to learn about this place than the interview showed. Ask before you sign.

## The other side {#flip-side}

A pomegranate can stay closed. Knowledge hoarded, feelings never cut open, the friend who knows everything and tells you nothing. The High Priestess’s minus in my notes is exactly that: she shows a façade, not the rest.

On the Empress the shadow is the reverse: too much fruit. In my notes she can smother with her care, or stop feeding. Plenty you aren’t allowed to refuse becomes a weight.

## With the other lenses {#lenses}

A pomegranate is a detail; read it after the colour, the number and the element — [the complete path](/learn-tarot/) gives the order. Then open [the Empress](/library/tarot-cards/the-empress/) next to the High Priestess and look at where each woman keeps her fruit.
