## What the lion says {#definition}

A lion is strength that doesn’t need to prove itself — until it does. **The lion says: there’s a powerful, hot energy here — courage, pride, desire — and the question is who’s holding it.** Think of a big dog pulling on its lead: the same energy can make the walk a joy or drag you into the road.

Its mane already looks like a crown, which is why it ends up on thrones and coats of arms. It’s the animal of whoever wants to be seen as strong.

## Where it appears in the deck {#where}

Strength is the lion’s card: a woman in white bends over a big red lion and closes its jaws without effort. The lion isn’t killed or chained; it’s calmed. [The Strength page](/library/tarot-cards/strength/) reads the whole card, and [Leo](/library/astrology/leo/) the sign behind it.

The Wheel of Fortune and the World both keep a lion in the bottom right corner — winged and reading a book on the Wheel, a head in the clouds on the World. It’s one of four creatures, with the bull, the eagle and the human figure, usually read as the four [fixed](/library/astrological-modes/fixed/) signs.

The Queen of Wands sits on a throne with lions’ heads carved on its arms and lions on its back. The King of Wands has black lions on his throne, among salamanders. The lion is the Wands’ animal: the suit’s fire made visible.

On the Two of Cups, a red lion’s head with wide wings floats above the caduceus between the couple.

## How to read it {#reading}

When a lion appears, look at what’s being done with it. Calmed, on Strength: courage that doesn’t shout. Carved into a throne, on the Wands court: passion turned into authority. Watching from a corner, on the Wheel and the World: steady energy that holds while everything turns.

Lenses: the lion is [Fire](/library/four-elements/fire-element/) — creation, impulse, the energy that gets you moving, and that spreads when nobody masters it. In my notes, Fire’s archetype is a teenager full of life who thinks they can do anything. Colour: Strength’s lion is red-orange, and [orange](/library/tarot-colours/orange/) in my grid is creativity and desire.

Say someone draws Strength next to the King of Wands. Two lions: one calmed, one on a throne. The reading might be that you’ve learned to hold your temper, and now people are asking you to lead.

## The other side {#flip-side}

The lion’s flip side is pride: the roar for the audience, the leader who needs to be admired, the anger that comes out all at once. Leo’s verb is “I will”; at its worst, “I will” stops listening.

There’s an opposite shadow too: the lion you never let out. Courage swallowed so often that you forget you have any.

## With the other lenses {#lenses}

A lion is a detail; read it after the colour, the number and the element — [the complete path](/learn-tarot/) gives the order. Then look at the [Queen of Wands](/library/tarot-cards/queen-of-wands/): her lions are carved, and the black cat at her feet is real.
