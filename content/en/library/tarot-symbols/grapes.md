## What grapes say {#definition}

Grapes are a harvest you can’t rush. A new vine takes years to bear well, the bunches take a summer to ripen, and the wine needs more time again. **Grapes say: something you’ve tended for a long time is ready, and it’s meant to be enjoyed, not only stored.** Think of a dinner where the friend who made the wine pours it herself.

They’re also a fruit for sharing. Nobody eats a bunch of grapes in one bite.

## Where it appears in the deck {#where}

On [the King of Pentacles](/library/tarot-cards/king-of-pentacles/), grapes are everywhere. His dark robe is covered with vines and bunches of grapes, more vines climb around a throne carved with bulls’ heads, and a castle stands behind him. The most prosperous-looking card in the deck is dressed as a vineyard.

On the Nine of Pentacles, a woman stands in a garden of vines heavy with grapes, a hooded bird on her gloved hand. In my notes, this card is enjoying what comes, with gratitude for everything you have.

On the Ten of Pentacles, bunches of grapes are part of the pattern on the robe of the old man in the foreground, among the family, the dogs and the archway. The harvest handed down.

On the Three of Cups, the woman on the right holds a bunch of grapes in her lowered hand, and more grapes lie at the women’s feet beside a pumpkin and other fruit. The harvest as a party.

## How to read it {#reading}

When grapes come up, ask whether a harvest is ready, and whether anyone is letting themselves enjoy it. On the Pentacles, grapes are wealth that has been grown: the King wears it, the woman of the Nine walks in it, the old man of the Ten has passed it on. On the Three of Cups, they’re pleasure shared with friends.

Lenses: three of the four cards are [Earth](/library/four-elements/earth-element/), the Pentacles. In my notes Earth is matter, prosperity, whatever has value for you, money or sentimental — and not all riches are gold. Number: 9 is the finish line, 10 the passage to what comes next, and [3](/library/numerology/number-3/) is a good rhythm, the machine running.

Say someone draws the King of Pentacles after years of building a small business. The grapes might say: this is the harvest. Count it, then let yourself taste it.

## The other side {#flip-side}

Grapes can be left on the vine. The person who works for the harvest and never stops to enjoy it; the savings nobody may touch. Or the reverse: a harvest celebrated before it’s in, the Three of Cups still dancing while the work isn’t finished. In my notes, the minus of 3 is starting new projects before the others are done.

And ripe fruit doesn’t keep. What isn’t picked in time spoils on the stem.

## With the other lenses {#lenses}

Grapes are a detail; read them after the colour, the number and the element — [the complete path](/learn-tarot/) explains the order. Then look at [the Nine of Pentacles](/library/tarot-cards/nine-of-pentacles/): she stands among the vines, and picks nothing.
