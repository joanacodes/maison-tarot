## What the angel says {#definition}

An angel, on a card, is a winged figure set above the human ones. You don’t need to believe in angels to read it; look at where it is. **The angel says: there’s a wider view than the one you have from the ground.** Think of the friend who isn’t caught up in your argument and sees in two minutes what you couldn’t see in two weeks.

What the angel does with its hands tells you the rest. It pours, it calls, it opens its arms.

## Where it appears in the deck {#where}

Temperance has the deck’s most complete angel: red wings, a white robe, a triangle inside a square on the chest, one foot in the water and one on the bank, pouring from one cup into another. Balance worked at, drop by drop. [Its card page](/library/tarot-cards/temperance/) goes further.

On [the Lovers](/library/tarot-cards/the-lovers/), an angel with red wings and flame-like hair opens its arms over a naked man and woman, a huge sun behind it. The man looks at the woman; the woman looks up at the angel. The choice happens under its gaze.

On Judgement, an angel leans out of the clouds and blows a trumpet hung with a white banner and a red cross. Below, people rise from open coffins. The call you can’t pretend not to hear.

The Wheel of Fortune has a smaller one. In the top left corner, a winged figure reads a book on a cloud — one of four creatures, with an eagle, a bull and a lion, watching the wheel turn. The same four return on the World as heads in the clouds, without wings.

One comparison worth making: put the Lovers next to [the Devil](/library/tarot-cards/the-devil/). Same layout — a winged figure above a naked couple — but the wings are a bat’s, and the couple is chained.

## How to read it {#reading}

When an angel comes up, ask what it’s doing. Pouring, on Temperance: mixing, patience, the right dose. Presiding, on the Lovers: a choice made in the open. Calling, on Judgement: a decision you can’t put off any longer. Each time the angel is above the scene, not in it.

Colour: the three large angels have red wings, and red in [my grid](/library/tarot-colours/red/) is action and drive. These aren’t passive figures. Temperance’s white robe adds peace. Element: Temperance and Judgement are [Fire](/library/four-elements/fire-element/) cards, the Lovers Air. The angel brings energy and a clear head, not only softness.

## The other side {#flip-side}

The angel’s flip side is waiting to be rescued. A figure in the sky can become a reason not to act: someone will come, someone will decide, someone will put it right. The woman on the Lovers, looking up at the angel rather than at the man in front of her, can be read that way too.

And not everything is about spirits. Sometimes the wider view you need belongs to a friend, a doctor or a therapist, and that’s a perfectly good angel.

## With the other lenses {#lenses}

The angel is a detail; colour, number and element come first, and [the complete path](/learn-tarot/) explains the order. Then read [Judgement](/library/tarot-cards/judgement/) with the trumpet in mind: what call have you been pretending not to hear?
