## What it does in a chart {#chart}

Pluto says where you die and are reborn while alive: power, transformation, what you bury and what comes back up. Twenty years in each sign; a generational placement, and only its house is personal. Sign ruled: Scorpio.

## The cards that carry it {#cards}

Judgement, arcana XX: the graves opening, the call, what rises. Through Scorpio, Death. Two cards that frighten in the picture and say the same thing: something ends so that something else can begin. [The main card's page.](/library/tarot-cards/judgement/)

## What the test showed {#test}

Hard to test as such. But the House notes that Lilith and Chiron — two points tied to what Pluto describes, the wound and the shadow — held 75 to 90% in the test. Pluto may be too big for one life; its small cousins speak better.

A reminder of the frame: the host checked her chart box by box, then those of friends and strangers — a manual count, not a study. [The article about the test](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) and [the table of the twelve signs](/library/astrology/).
