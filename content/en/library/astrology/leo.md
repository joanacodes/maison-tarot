## In one line {#in-one-line}

**Youth, play, expressiveness, challenge, pride, innocence.** Verb: *I will*. Body: [Sun](/library/astrology/sun/). Element: [Fire](/library/four-elements/fire-element/). Mode: [fixed](/library/astrological-modes/fixed/). Energy: [masculine](/library/feminine-masculine-energies/). Colour: orange, gold, yellow. Animal: the big cats.

## What this energy does {#energy}

Leo energy is very demonstrative; it loves games and challenges. Think of children who adore playing, in sport as in art — and who want to be seen playing. Leo does nothing by halves, especially in front of an audience.

## Favourable sides, unfavourable sides {#flip-side}

**Favourable.** Demonstrative, playful, generous when watched.

**Unfavourable.** Self-centred, narcissistic.

## The archetype {#archetype}

The opera singer; kids playing Action Man.

## Its card: Strength {#card}

When Strength comes out, Leo is there: mastery through gentleness, the hand on the lion's jaw. Well placed, it's the courage that doesn't need to shout. Badly placed, it's the need to be seen coming before what you do. The card has [its own page](/library/tarot-cards/strength/), with all its lenses.

## What the House doesn't say {#limits}

This sign explains no one. The host tested her chart box by box: signs hold about one time in three — enough for a lens, not for a verdict. [The test, and its numbers.](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/)
