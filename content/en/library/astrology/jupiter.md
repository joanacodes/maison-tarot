## What it does in a chart {#chart}

Jupiter says where you're lucky, or what you take for luck: expansion, confidence, generosity, excess. It stays a year in each sign, so everyone born the same year shares yours — which limits what it can say about one person in particular. Sign ruled: Sagittarius.

## The cards that carry it {#cards}

[The Wheel of Fortune](/library/tarot-cards/wheel-of-fortune/), arcana X: what goes up, what goes down, and the fact that nothing stays. Through Sagittarius, Temperance. In the minors: the Six of Wands, the Nine of Cups, the Four and Eight of Swords, the Two of Pentacles.

## Not part of my test {#test}

Jupiter wasn't one of the placements I counted [when I tested my chart](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/), so I have no figure for it. I still add it when I read a chart, for a reason of my own that has nothing to do with luck: [where Jupiter sits is where I look for your biggest struggles](/blog/sun-moon-rising-big-three/#jupiter). Jupiter on a card is useful for one thing: recognising the Six, expansion, and remembering the advice that goes with it — stay humble.
