## What it does in a chart {#chart}

Jupiter says where you're lucky, or what you take for luck: expansion, confidence, generosity, excess. It stays a year in each sign, so everyone born the same year shares yours — which limits what it can say about one person in particular. Sign ruled: Sagittarius.

## The cards that carry it {#cards}

The Wheel of Fortune, arcana X: what goes up, what goes down, and the fact that nothing stays. Through Sagittarius, Temperance. In the minors: the Six of Wands, the Nine of Cups, the Four and Eight of Swords, the Two of Pentacles. [The main card's page.](/library/tarot-cards/wheel-of-fortune/)

## What the test showed {#test}

A third — and the House distrusts it more than the others, because it's the placement that makes people say 'it'll be fine'. Jupiter on a card is useful for one thing: recognising the Six, expansion, and remembering the advice that goes with it — stay humble.
