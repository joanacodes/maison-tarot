## What it does in a chart {#chart}

Saturn says the hardest lesson: where you're limited, required to prove yourself, late on what you wanted — and where, in time, you become solid precisely because it was hard. It's the placement of responsibility, of structure, of the work that takes years. Sign ruled: Capricorn.

## The cards that carry it {#cards}

The World, arcana XXI: the laurel wreath, the end of the cycle, what you ended up building. Through Capricorn, the Devil — Saturn when structure has become a chain. In the minors: the Five and Ten of Wands, the Eight of Cups, the Three of Swords, the Seven of Pentacles. [The main card's page.](/library/tarot-cards/the-world/)

## What the test showed {#test}

**75 to 90% in the test.** That's the result that made the House keep astrology. Wherever Saturn sat, the person recognised their hardest lesson almost every time, often in the exact words. The House reads Saturn as the World and the Devil: what you ended up building, and what chained you in the meantime.
