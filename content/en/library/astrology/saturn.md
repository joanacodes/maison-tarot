## What it does in a chart {#chart}

Saturn says the hardest lesson: where you're limited, required to prove yourself, late on what you wanted — and where, in time, you become solid precisely because it was hard. It's the placement of responsibility, of structure, of the work that takes years. Sign ruled: Capricorn.

## The cards that carry it {#cards}

[The World](/library/tarot-cards/the-world/), arcana XXI: the laurel wreath, the end of the cycle, what you ended up building. Through Capricorn, the Devil — Saturn when structure has become a chain. In the minors: the Five and Ten of Wands, the Eight of Cups, the Three of Swords, the Seven of Pentacles.

## What the test showed {#test}

**Saturn held [75 to 90% of the time](/library/astrology/#the-test).** That's the result that made me keep astrology. Wherever Saturn sat, the person recognised their hardest lesson almost every time, often in the exact words. I read Saturn as the World and the Devil: what you ended up building, and what chained you in the meantime.
