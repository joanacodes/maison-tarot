## What it does in a chart {#chart}

Neptune says where you dissolve: dream, intuition, illusion, the blur between yourself and the rest. Fourteen years in each sign — a generational placement, and the hardest of all to check, because what it describes won't be described. Sign ruled: Pisces.

## The cards that carry it {#cards}

The Hanged Man, arcana XII: suspended, head down, seeing the world the other way up. Through Pisces, the Moon. Two cards of inverted perspective, not of misfortune. [The main card's page.](/library/tarot-cards/the-hanged-man/)

## What the test showed {#test}

Hard to test, and the House is careful with it for a precise reason: it's the placement most used to excuse what people don't want to look at. 'It's my Neptune.' No. The House reads it as the Hanged Man: a viewpoint to turn over, not a fog to live in.

A reminder of the frame: the host checked her chart box by box, then those of friends and strangers — a manual count, not a study. [The article about the test](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) and [the table of the twelve signs](/library/astrology/).
