## What it does in a chart {#chart}

Uranus says where you break: freedom taken rather than asked for, the unforeseen, invention, what arrives at once and doesn't go back into place. It stays seven years in each sign, so it's a generational placement more than a personal one; its house, though, is individual. Sign ruled: Aquarius.

## The cards that carry it {#cards}

The Fool, arcana 0: the step above the drop, the departure nothing justifies. Through Aquarius, the Star. A planet discovered in 1781; its card attributions are modern, and the House takes them for what they are — a useful convention. [The main card's page.](/library/tarot-cards/the-fool/)

## What the test showed {#test}

Hard to test: what Uranus describes rarely happens at the moment you check. The House uses it to read the Fool — the leap — and asks nothing more of it.

A reminder of the frame: the host checked her chart box by box, then those of friends and strangers — a manual count, not a study. [The article about the test](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) and [the table of the twelve signs](/library/astrology/).
