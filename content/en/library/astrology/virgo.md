## In one line {#in-one-line}

**Service, trade and communication, pragmatism.** Verb: *I analyse*. Body: [Mercury](/library/astrology/mercury/). Element: [Earth](/library/four-elements/earth-element/). Mode: [mutable](/library/astrological-modes/mutable/). Energy: [feminine](/library/feminine-masculine-energies/). Colour: blue, grey. Animal: domestic animals.

## What this energy does {#energy}

Virgo energy — not to be confused with the Virgin Mary — is solitary by nature, and yet wants to be of service to someone, or something, bigger than itself. It wants so badly to be useful that it doesn't always ask whom it works for: the lawyer of a killer who has no intention of stopping, the researchers funded by the army and surprised at the use made of their work. People say it manipulates without knowing; in truth it knows most of the time and chooses to lie to itself — where Capricorn, at least in its own head, owns it. Better to have it beside you than against you.

## Favourable sides, unfavourable sides {#flip-side}

**Favourable.** A very good observer, an excellent executor; the perfect element in group work, finding solutions even in a complicated situation.

**Unfavourable.** Amorality, manipulation it tells itself is unconscious.

## The archetype {#archetype}

Jinx in Arcane; the engineer, the adviser to the throne, the manager.

## Its card: The Hermit {#card}

When the Hermit comes out, Virgo is there: someone has withdrawn to understand, analyses before acting, lights one step at a time. Well placed, it's the solution nobody saw. Badly placed, it's service rendered to the wrong cause. The card has [its own page](/library/tarot-cards/the-hermit/), with all its lenses.
