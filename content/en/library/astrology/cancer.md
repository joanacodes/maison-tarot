## In one line {#in-one-line}

**Nurturer, director, protector, very strong intuition.** Verb: *I feel*. Body: [Moon](/library/astrology/moon/). Element: [Water](/library/four-elements/water-element/). Mode: [cardinal](/library/astrological-modes/cardinal/). Energy: [feminine](/library/feminine-masculine-energies/). Colour: green and silver. Animal: crabs and crustaceans, the octopus.

## What this energy does {#energy}

Two people lift weights that look identical; the first one's weigh 4 kilos, the other's 78. The first can't understand why the other makes so much noise lifting. Some say nothing; some call it drama. In time the second learns to lift silently. Then one day the first picks up the wrong weights, and understands. This is Cancer energy: it can whine, but it's tough — it absorbs and contains what others don't see, and meanwhile it does the job.

## Favourable sides, unfavourable sides {#flip-side}

**Favourable.** Patience. The one who makes the home a place worth living in.

**Unfavourable.** Victim of theft, emotional yo-yo.

## The archetype {#archetype}

The mother or the father at home. Hodor, in Game of Thrones: the one who holds the door.

## Its card: The Chariot {#card}

When [the Chariot](/library/tarot-cards/the-chariot/) comes out, Cancer is there: a hard shell around a soft inside, a warrior in armour who moves forward under weights nobody sees. Well placed, it's the will that protects and keeps going. Badly placed, it's the armour that never comes off. Cancer doesn't take; it gives.
