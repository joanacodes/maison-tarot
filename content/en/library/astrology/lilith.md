## What it does in a chart {#chart}

Lilith — the Black Moon, in modern astrology — isn't a body: it's a calculated point, the apogee of the Moon's orbit. It says what you learned to hide: the desire you don't admit, the anger you put away, the part of yourself you excluded in order to be accepted. Where it sits, the person was required to keep quiet. Sign ruled: no sign — a point, not a body.

## The cards that carry it {#cards}

No Golden Dawn attribution — Lilith postdates the system. The House reads it with the Devil, arcana XV: the chains you could remove, what you let be chained, the appetite you decided to despise. And sometimes with the Eight of Swords: the one who believes herself prisoner of a bond she never checked. [The main card's page.](/library/tarot-cards/the-devil/)

## What the test showed {#test}

**75 to 90% in the test.** With Saturn and Chiron, the placement that held best — often the hardest to say out loud, because the person knew it and had never heard anyone say it. The House reads Lilith with care: it's a lens that opens a door closed on purpose. You knock first.

A reminder of the frame: the host checked her chart box by box, then those of friends and strangers — a manual count, not a study. [The article about the test](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) and [the table of the twelve signs](/library/astrology/).
