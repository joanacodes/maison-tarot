Each sign will have its page, with its keywords, its favourable and unfavourable sides, its archetype and its animal — from the host's notes, which are not horoscopes.

## What the test showed {#the-test}

The host took her birth chart and checked it box by box, the way you check an invoice. Overall: 30 to 40% accurate. For three bodies in particular — Saturn, Lilith, Chiron — 75 to 90%. That's little and a lot at once: enough to keep astrology as a lens, not enough to hand it a decision. The detail is on the [Method](/method/) page, and the blog tells the test body by body.

## How to lay this lens on a card {#how}

The twelve major arcana above carry a sign: when you draw one, you get its verb, its element and its mode at once. The Emperor *is* — Fire, cardinal: he starts and he commands. The High Priestess *feels* — Water, cardinal: she initiates in silence.

The cards from 2 to 10 carry a planet in a sign (the decans). It's a finer layer, noted on every [card page](/library/tarot-cards/), useful when the first three lenses no longer settle it.

## The twelve bodies {#the-bodies}

Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto — and Lilith and Chiron, which earned their page in the test. Each will have its own: what it does to a sign, which cards carry it, and why the House trusts it or not.
