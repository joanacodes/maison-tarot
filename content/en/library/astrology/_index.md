## What the test showed {#the-test}

I took my birth chart and checked it box by box, the way you check an invoice; then I did the same with the charts of friends and strangers — a manual count, not a study. Overall: 30 to 40% accurate. For three bodies in particular — Saturn, Lilith, Chiron — 75 to 90%. That's little and a lot at once: enough to keep astrology as a lens, not enough to hand it a decision. The detail sits in [the method write-up](/method/) and in [the article that tells the test](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/); the blog then takes it body by body.

That frame holds for every sign and every body in this section. **No sign explains anyone, not even yours.**

## How to lay this lens on a card {#how}

The twelve major arcana above carry a sign: when you draw one, you get its verb, its element and its mode at once. The Emperor *is* — Fire, cardinal: he starts and he commands. The Chariot *feels* — Water, cardinal: he sets off with his feelings held steady.

Each numbered minor, Two to Ten, holds one planet placed in one sign: its decan. It's a finer layer, noted on every [card page](/library/tarot-cards/), useful when the first three lenses no longer settle it.

## The twelve bodies {#the-bodies}

Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto — and Lilith and Chiron, which earned their page in the test. Each has its own: what it does to a sign, which cards carry it, and why I trust it or not.
