## In one line {#in-one-line}

**Calm, focus, wisdom, listening, emotion, help — and the strange, the mysterious, the withdrawn.** Verb: *I believe*. Body: [Neptune](/library/astrology/neptune/). Element: [Water](/library/four-elements/water-element/). Mode: [mutable](/library/astrological-modes/mutable/). Energy: [feminine](/library/feminine-masculine-energies/). Colour: turquoise, pale green. Animal: fish.

## What this energy does {#energy}

A fish lives in water, not on land breathing oxygen: in an environment humans can't live in. That's what to understand about Pisces — the calmest energy in the zodiac, solitary and social at once. From outside, the surface is still; underneath, the world teems with life and creatures. This energy is wise in the precise sense that it keeps itself: it says nothing until the conversation deserves it, and speaks when it's needed. Its opinion is rare, therefore good. The risk is that the surface ends up hiding everything: self-erasure, then a depth that no longer comes up.

## Favourable sides, unfavourable sides {#flip-side}

**Favourable.** Friendly, a listener, knows how to keep itself: doesn't spend energy on a pointless conversation, but gives its view when it matters — and that view, rare, is precious.

**Unfavourable.** Depression, self-erasure.

## The archetype {#archetype}

The person who's always calm, always there, and whom you discover years later knew everything.

## Its card: The Moon {#card}

[The Moon](/library/tarot-cards/the-moon/) is Pisces' card: what happens under the surface, the intuition that has no words yet, sometimes illusion. Its best side knows how to wait until the moment is right to speak; its worst has gone down so deep that nobody knows when it will surface.
