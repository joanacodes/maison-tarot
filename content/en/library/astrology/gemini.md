## In one line {#in-one-line}

**Communication, exchange, relay, connection.** Verb: *I think*. Body: [Mercury](/library/astrology/mercury/). Element: [Air](/library/four-elements/air-element/). Mode: [mutable](/library/astrological-modes/mutable/). Energy: [masculine](/library/feminine-masculine-energies/). Colour: blue. Animal: brightly coloured birds and butterflies.

## What this energy does {#energy}

Gemini energy is the ace of communication. Balanced, it expresses its thoughts and feelings in the most exact way — and can, in the same way, translate and transcribe what someone else meant. Out of balance it isn't neutral: it makes the situation worse, often unintentionally, sometimes not.

## Favourable sides, unfavourable sides {#flip-side}

**Favourable.** Adapts, communicates.

**Unfavourable.** Lies, gossip, misunderstanding, adding fuel to the fire.

## The archetype {#archetype}

The very good reporter, the lawyer — or the game of telephone.

## Its card: The Lovers {#card}

When [the Lovers](/library/tarot-cards/the-lovers/) come out, Gemini is talking: a choice between two, a conversation that decides, a relay. Well placed, it's the right word that unites. Badly placed, it's the version going round that's no longer the true one.
