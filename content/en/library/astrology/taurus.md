## In one line {#in-one-line}

**Guardian energy, beauty, stability.** Verb: *I have*. Body: [Venus](/library/astrology/venus/). Element: [Earth](/library/four-elements/earth-element/). Mode: [fixed](/library/astrological-modes/fixed/). Energy: [feminine](/library/feminine-masculine-energies/). Colour: green, pale blue, mauve. Animal: the cow, the bull.

## What this energy does {#energy}

Taurus energy has a sense of beauty and aims for a happy ending at any cost — which makes it very patient and strong over time. It's also why it's hard to get it to consider something else, change its mind or try something new. Which, in the long run, can work against it.

## Favourable sides, unfavourable sides {#flip-side}

**Favourable.** Patience, the nurturer, keeps well what it holds dear, strength over the long run.

**Unfavourable.** Stubborn and obstinate, closed to change, possessive.

## The archetype {#archetype}

The keeper of the garden, the one who maintains; conservative Taurus, the opposite of Aquarius who wants the new.

## Its card: The Hierophant {#card}

[The Hierophant](/library/tarot-cards/the-hierophant/) brings Taurus in with something handed down and kept — a trade, a method, a tradition, the way things have always been done. At best, knowledge that lasts because someone looked after it; at worst, a rule followed out of habit, and the change Taurus refuses to consider.
