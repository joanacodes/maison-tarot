## In one line {#in-one-line}

**Travel, curiosity, sharing, learning.** Verb: *I see, I try*. Body: [Jupiter](/library/astrology/jupiter/). Element: [Fire](/library/four-elements/fire-element/). Mode: [mutable](/library/astrological-modes/mutable/). Energy: [masculine](/library/feminine-masculine-energies/). Colour: purple. Animal: the horse, the centaur.

## What this energy does {#energy}

Sagittarius energy loves to learn from others and to share what it has learned. A great traveller, Sagittarius gathers knowledge by leaving, then tries to mix it to see what it gives together. It adapts to its surroundings in order to learn, will go to opposing camps to hear every side, and judges more slowly than the others. One of the most sociable signs of the zodiac.

## Favourable sides, unfavourable sides {#flip-side}

**Favourable.** Loves learning from others and sharing what it learned; judges less readily than other signs; one of the most sociable.

**Unfavourable.** The scattering of someone who tastes everything and settles nowhere.

## The archetype {#archetype}

The one who leaves to drive round the world in a van, goes to India, tries the unknown food.

## Its card: Temperance {#card}

When Temperance comes out, Sagittarius is there: two things being blended into a third, a measure found on the road. Well placed, it's the alloy that holds. Badly placed, it's one blend too many, or the departure that avoids finishing. The card has [its own page](/library/tarot-cards/temperance/), with all its lenses.

## What the House doesn't say {#limits}

This sign explains no one. The host tested her chart box by box: signs hold about one time in three — enough for a lens, not for a verdict. [The test, and its numbers.](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/)
