## In one line {#in-one-line}

**Leadership, calm, teaching, work.** Verb: *I use*. Body: [Saturn](/library/astrology/saturn/). Element: [Earth](/library/four-elements/earth-element/). Mode: [cardinal](/library/astrological-modes/cardinal/). Energy: [feminine](/library/feminine-masculine-energies/). Colour: black, brown. Animal: the goat.

## What this energy does {#energy}

Capricorn energy is solitary and stops for no one, because it's focused on the task. Capricorn likes to know itself independent and avoids binding contracts; time is precious to it. It appeals strongly to everyone's responsibility, starting with its own — which drives it to keep asking what it could do better, to question itself constantly, and sometimes to stall, or to lose itself in the effort to the point of no longer living in the present. Like Virgo, an excellent observer; out of balance, a very good manipulator — and it knows.

## Favourable sides, unfavourable sides {#flip-side}

**Favourable.** Persevering and patient, speaks only to say constructive things; knows how to use every tool and enjoys passing them on.

**Unfavourable.** Self-centred, selfish, conscious manipulation.

## The archetype {#archetype}

Jon Snow in Game of Thrones; Miranda Priestly in The Devil Wears Prada; army chiefs, chief executives.

## Its card: The Devil {#card}

When [the Devil](/library/tarot-cards/the-devil/) comes out, Capricorn is there: ambition, structure, and the chains that come with them — which can be removed, if you look closely. Well placed, it's responsibility taken. Badly placed, it's the tool become the master.
