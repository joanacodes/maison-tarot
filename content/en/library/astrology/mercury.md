## What it does in a chart {#chart}

Mercury says how you think and how you speak: the speed of the mind, the taste for words, the way you learn. It never gets more than one sign away from the Sun, so it often confirms what the Sun already said — which partly explains why it tests like it. Sign ruled: Gemini and Virgo.

## The cards that carry it {#cards}

The Magician, arcana I: the four tools on the table, the raised hand, the intelligence that connects. Through Gemini, the Lovers; through Virgo, the Hermit. In the minors: the Eight of Wands, the Three of Cups, the Six of Swords, the Five and Ten of Pentacles. [The main card's page.](/library/tarot-cards/the-magician/)

## What the test showed {#test}

A third accurate. The House uses it for one precise thing: knowing whether a person reads a card with words or with eyes. A Mercury in Air comments; a Mercury in Earth checks. Useful in a workshop, not in a reading.

A reminder of the frame: the host checked her chart box by box, then those of friends and strangers — a manual count, not a study. [The article about the test](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) and [the table of the twelve signs](/library/astrology/).
