## What it does in a chart {#chart}

Chiron is a small body between Saturn and Uranus, discovered in 1977, named after the centaur who healed others of a wound he couldn't heal in himself. In astrology it says exactly that: the wound you circle all your life, and what you become able to heal in others because of it. Sign ruled: no sign — an asteroid.

## The cards that carry it {#cards}

No old attribution. The House reads it with the Hermit, arcana IX: the one who withdrew, who carries a lantern, and lights others' way with what he learned from his own night. And sometimes with the Three of Swords, the clean wound, and the Six of Swords, the crossing that leaves it behind. [The main card's page.](/library/tarot-cards/the-hermit/)

## What the test showed {#test}

**75 to 90% in the test.** The third of the three placements that held. The House notices that Saturn, Lilith and Chiron all speak of a difficulty — the lesson, the hidden, the wound — and that this may be what people know best about themselves. It uses it for one thing: reminding you that a good reader reads with their own wound, not in spite of it.

A reminder of the frame: the host checked her chart box by box, then those of friends and strangers — a manual count, not a study. [The article about the test](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) and [the table of the twelve signs](/library/astrology/).
