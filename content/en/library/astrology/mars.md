## What it does in a chart {#chart}

Mars says how you act and how you fight: raw energy, anger, desire, the way you take what you want. It's the placement of the body that moves, and it shows from outside more than most — perhaps why it held better in the test than the Sun. Sign ruled: Aries.

## The cards that carry it {#cards}

The Tower, arcana XVI: the lightning, the falling crown, what collapses at once. Through Aries, the Emperor — Mars in its built version. In the minors: the Two and Seven of Wands, the Five and Ten of Cups, the Nine of Swords, the Three of Pentacles. [The main card's page.](/library/tarot-cards/the-tower/)

## What the test showed {#test}

A little under one in two: better than average. The House reads Mars as the Tower and the Emperor together — the same force, depending on whether it has a plan. On a Wands card, Mars says speed; on a Swords card, harshness.

A reminder of the frame: the host checked her chart box by box, then those of friends and strangers — a manual count, not a study. [The article about the test](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) and [the table of the twelve signs](/library/astrology/).
