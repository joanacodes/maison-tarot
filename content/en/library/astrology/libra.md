## In one line {#in-one-line}

**Diplomacy.** Verb: *I balance*. Body: [Venus](/library/astrology/venus/). Element: [Air](/library/four-elements/air-element/). Mode: [cardinal](/library/astrological-modes/cardinal/). Energy: [masculine](/library/feminine-masculine-energies/). Colour: blue and lavender blue. Animal: the snake, the lizard.

## What this energy does {#energy}

Libra energy wants to be fair and seeks balance at any cost. In a conflict it tends towards total impartiality: it would like every side to get the same result, the same amount. That's its strength and its trap: faced with a real dilemma, the kind where no pan can be level, it can freeze.

## Favourable sides, unfavourable sides {#flip-side}

**Favourable.** Wants to be fair; seeks balance at any cost; impartial in a conflict, because it wants nobody to walk away with less than the other.

**Unfavourable.** Indecision — paralysed before an impossible dilemma.

## The archetype {#archetype}

The diplomat; the referee; the one who redoes the split until nobody is short-changed — and hasn't decided yet.

## Its card: Justice {#card}

[Justice](/library/tarot-cards/justice/) puts Libra in front of a decision to be made fairly, an account to be settled evenly. Either the judgment comes out clear, or the scale keeps swinging and nobody reads it.
