## What it does in a chart {#chart}

Venus says what you love and how you love: taste, attraction, the way you bond, what you find beautiful. It's the placement everyone wants read first in a love reading — and the one it's easiest to say something flattering and empty about. Sign ruled: Taurus and Libra.

## The cards that carry it {#cards}

The Empress, arcana III: Venus is drawn on her shield, literally. Through Taurus, the same Empress; through Libra, Justice. In the minors: the Four of Wands, the Two and Seven of Cups, the Five of Swords, the Nine of Pentacles. [The main card's page.](/library/tarot-cards/the-empress/)

## What the test showed {#test}

A third accurate, and one rule: the House doesn't read 'does he love me' with Venus or with anything else. Venus is for reading a Cups card — the Two of Cups is ruled by Venus, and it shows — not for predicting a feeling.

A reminder of the frame: the host checked her chart box by box, then those of friends and strangers — a manual count, not a study. [The article about the test](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/) and [the table of the twelve signs](/library/astrology/).
