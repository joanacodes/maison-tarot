## In one line {#in-one-line}

**Intensity, observation, healing — and the sting.** Verb: *I desire*. Body: [Pluto](/library/astrology/pluto/). Element: [Water](/library/four-elements/water-element/). Mode: [fixed](/library/astrological-modes/fixed/). Energy: [feminine](/library/feminine-masculine-energies/). Colour: burgundy, dark brown. Animal: insects and crustaceans.

## What this energy does {#energy}

Scorpio energy is very intense, and Scorpio knows exactly where to sting so that it hurts. All its power is in the sting, which makes it frightening — and that fear is what drives some people to duplicity to get what they want. So Scorpio becomes wary and observant; on the wheel, it's one of the signs that attracts the most adrenaline-seekers, without having asked for it. This energy can kill as much as it can heal.

## Favourable sides, unfavourable sides {#flip-side}

**Favourable.** A very good observer, a very good healer.

**Unfavourable.** Toxic relationships, a tendency to isolate.

## The archetype {#archetype}

The person who says nothing at the party and draws everyone's attention without meaning to. Usually has a scorpion tattoo, without knowing why.

## Its card: Death {#card}

When Death comes out, Scorpio is there: a transformation carried through, wanted or not. Well placed, it's the healing that first requires something to end. Badly placed, it's the sting turned on oneself. The card has [its own page](/library/tarot-cards/death/), with all its lenses.

## What the House doesn't say {#limits}

This sign explains no one. The host tested her chart box by box: signs hold about one time in three — enough for a lens, not for a verdict. [The test, and its numbers.](/blog/i-tested-my-birth-chart/)
