## An example: communicate, then act

In a delicate conversation with someone closed-off, feminine energy helps: it takes several paths, uses metaphors, gets the point across. But when a quick decision is needed, that same energy can lead to anxiety and paralysis.

Masculine energy is direct — sometimes brutal in how it explains, to the point of not landing the message, or closing the other person even further. On the other hand it decides fast, whether the decision is good or bad.

**In short: feminine energy to negotiate and build a plan; masculine energy on the battlefield, to run the plan — and try something else if it fails.**

## Like yin and yang

Each energy holds a piece of the other. They’re equal: neither sits above, each has its helpful sides and its downsides. Feminine energy is the energy of the intangible, of what isn’t seen; masculine energy is the one that shows itself.

| Feminine energy | Masculine energy |
| --- | --- |
| To think | To act |
| Patience | Impatience |
| Psychological violence | Physical violence |
| Art | Science |
| Intangible | Tangible |
| Imagination | Construction |
| To hide | To show |
| Backstage | On stage |
| Electron | Proton |
| Gentleness | Brutality |

## Why the line is never clean

A designer uses art (feminine energy) and her knowledge of calculation (masculine energy) to create a product meant to be consumed or used — a tangible end. An artist produces for intangible ends: her own satisfaction, or the client’s, without the work being used the way you use a fork. And yet an artist who creates a tarot deck makes cards to be *used*. Everything overlaps, mixes, completes. That’s exactly what makes the lens useful: it doesn’t sort people, it says *which way a card leans, at that moment*.

## On the cards

Every [element](/library/four-elements/) leans: Earth and Water towards the feminine, Fire and Air towards the masculine. Every zodiac sign too — the [astrology](/library/astrology/) page goes through them. When you hold an unknown card, ask first: is this showing itself, or hiding? You already have half the reading.
