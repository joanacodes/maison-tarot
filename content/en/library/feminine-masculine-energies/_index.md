## Not about women and men {#definition}

Feminine and masculine energies aren’t about being a woman or a man. They’re dynamics, like yin and yang: each holds a part of the other, they’re equal in value, and each has its good sides and its less good ones. **We all carry both, in different proportions — seventy-thirty one way, sixty-forty the other — and the mix can shift.**

## Two ways of moving through the world {#two-ways}

Feminine energy is thinking, patience, imagination, art, the intangible. It hides, it works behind the scenes, it’s gentle — and when it turns violent, the violence is psychological. In conversation it takes detours and metaphors to approach a delicate subject: very good for negotiating and planning, and it can freeze when a quick decision is needed.

Masculine energy is acting, impatience, science, the tangible, building. It shows itself, it’s on stage — and when it turns violent, the violence is physical, its flip side brutality. In conversation it goes straight to the point and can close people off, but it decides fast: good on the battlefield, following the plan.

## Why the line is never clean {#overlap}

A designer uses art (feminine) and calculation (masculine) to make a product people will use. An artist makes things for intangible ends. And someone who designs a tarot deck makes art that is meant to be used. Everything overlaps — which is exactly what makes this a useful lens rather than a way of sorting people.

## On the cards {#cards}

Every card leans one way. When you hold a card you don’t know, ask one question first: is this showing itself, or hiding? You already have half the reading.
