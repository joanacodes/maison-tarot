## What a session isn’t

It isn’t a psychic consultation, and it isn’t an appointment where someone decides for you. The host reads; you decide. Everything said in a session is offered for entertainment, and you remain responsible for your choices — it’s written everywhere because it’s true.

## Who it’s for

The curious, the candid, people who ask questions and don’t drink the host’s words as truth. If you want to feel above others because you know a bit about the cards, this isn’t the house. If you’re looking for someone to replace your therapist, neither.
